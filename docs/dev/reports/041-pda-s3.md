## 1. Summary

- **Built:** the band at the bottom of the portrait panel (key strip, soft buttons Home / Menu / Apps / Keys, the
  letters and digits boxes), drawn as a third LVGL display under the app area by the display backend; every touch
  routed by where its first contact lands (pen strokes to the recogniser, never to the app); what the recogniser
  returns typed through `cp0_keyboard_inject_key()` (US keys, and text alone for characters without a key: `£`, `é`,
  `©`); the ink of a stroke, its fade, the red flash of a refused stroke, the mode line (abc / Abc / ABC, Shift, Caps,
  Punct, Ext, a pending accent); the key strip's sticky Ctrl / Shift / Alt, Tab, held arrows with repeat, Enter;
  `input.state vkb=zone` while the launcher is in front and the usual rule while a (turned) fullscreen app is; the
  Strokes library moved into `cp0_lvgl` (the tools build the same files); the tables shipped in
  `/usr/share/APPLaunch/strokes`; the trademark check in `check-branding.sh`; `APPLAUNCH_PDA=1` in the
  `pi3a-wsdsi43-portrait` profile.
- **Tests:** `test_zone.c` (geometry, hit test, router, strip state machine, result mapping with non-ASCII, mode line,
  the whole path through the real recogniser), `vkb=zone` in three existing tests, the panel / app-area split in
  `test_app_launch_env`, 5 harness scenes with 30 goldens at 480x640 and 480x800, a strokes log replayed by the S1
  tools; `check-all.sh` all steps PASS (a new step `strokes` = `make -C tools/strokes check`).
- **Goldens:** every existing golden (117: 77 at 640x480 / 480x320, 40 PDA) and every one of the 1885 PNGs the S2 tree
  rendered byte-identical; 30 new (section 7).
- **After the first board test (pda.4, section 12):** b and a plain V (no flick) were refused or read as d / u: the
  table had only the chart's stroke of each; b and v now have natural variants (b 6, v 3, plus a narrow u that keeps
  u apart), told apart from u by a new gate `sharp=` (the stroke's sharpest turn, Strokes 0.2.1). Synthetic accuracy
  unchanged (99.54 / 0.41 / 0.05 %, harsh 82.6 / 14.8 / 2.6 %). The writing zone is a third of the panel's height
  (267 px at 480x800, 213 at 480x640); at 480x640 the app area (480x374) drops to the Compact class and a long page
  title is cut (section 12.3, a cap proposed, not applied).
- **Left for later:** Settings > Strokes on/off (the profile's `APPLAUNCH_PDA=0` instead), keys from the zone to a
  compat app through `applaunch-vkbd` (compat apps are hidden on portrait), portrait apps drawing the zone in their own
  process (S4: `APPLAUNCH_ZONE`, `screen.state` `orient=` / `zone=`), the training screen (S6), a key click sound, an
  echo of the last character.

```
+-------------------------------+ 0                     |
| app area: the native display  |                deliver_frame (dpi backend)
| 480x482, its own metrics      |                 |-- cp0_zone_route(): owner of the touch by its first contact

+-------------------------------+ 800  panel: 267)
```

## 3. Decisions taken where the brief was silent (the owner may change any)

| # | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | Sizes from the mockup: soft buttons and strip keys 5 mm high (43 px on the DSI, the mockup's 41), the writing zone with its 8 px padding a third of the panel's height (267 px at 480x800; owner after pda.4, section 12; first 20.6 mm, 176 px, the owner's earlier figure), boxes inset by `SPACE_S`, letters 68 % of the boxes' width; strip key widths in the mockup's proportions (Tab 1.3, Ctrl 1.5, Shift 1.5, Alt 1.2, arrows 1, Enter 1.8) | the owner chose variant A; 5 mm is under the 7 mm TARGET rule: one constant each (`CP0_ZONE_KEY_MM_X10`, `CP0_ZONE_WRITE_DIV`) |
| D2 | The app area gets its own metrics (480x482 at 8.54 px/mm: class Square, header 64 px instead of S2's 56; 480x374 at 9.04: class Compact, see section 12.3); `screen.state`, the turned and fullscreen apps' canvas, `X-MinSize` / `X-Portrait`, the multitasking rule and G1 keep the panel | report 038 measured the app areas as Square and found them correct; the home grid must fit the area |
| D3 | The open strip does not resize the native display: it covers its bottom 51 px, and every screen that follows the on-screen keyboard's inset (`Page` bodies, the terminal, dialogs) leaves that row free; the home grid and other screens do not follow the inset and are covered there | resizing the display at run time would need every page rebuilt; the inset path exists and is tested |
| D4 | Modifiers: the on-screen keyboard's state machine (tap arms, a second tap within 400 ms locks, a tap turns off); an armed one is used by the next strip key **or** recognised character; Shift also applies to keys (Shift+Tab, Shift+arrows) and upper-cases letters (Latin-1 too); Ctrl+character sends the key code with Ctrl and no text (Ctrl+C), and a character without a US key is typed with Ctrl still armed; Alt is a real key (its press when armed, its release after the next key: Alt, Tab = a quick Alt+Tab), as on the terminal's keyboard. Armed = blue (the mockup's Ctrl), locked = light grey with a dark label | the owner's "tap again = off, double tap lock"; one rule for the OSK and the strip |
| D5 | The strip stays open across pages until Keys; it closes by itself when the multitasking switcher comes up (its Home / Close all bar would be under the strip) | the owner's "stays open until Keys"; the switcher is the one screen it would hide controls of |
| D6 | Mode line: the letters box label is abc / Abc / ABC; a hint at the top right of the box of the last stroke says Shift, Caps, Punct, Ext, "Accent: acute" (words: the builtin font has no accent glyphs) | the brief lists the modes, not their place |
| D7 | Ink #FFBE00 (the mockup), 0.4 mm (3 px), fades over 260 ms after the result; a refused stroke turns red for 200 ms, then fades | the mockup's colour; 038's "short red flash" |
| D8 | Routing by the first contact of a touch; the writing zone's margins count as the nearest box; a stroke may leave its box (into the app area too); a press within the 50 ms heal window and 18 mm of a pen lift continues the stroke wherever it lands (so a drop-out never clicks the app); any second contact cancels the stroke; several fingers landing at once in the zone write nothing; the multitasking G1 / G2 still get every frame | the brief: "a 3-finger gesture is not a stroke"; S1's drop-out rule |
| D9 | Keys typed with the keyboard's control text (Enter "\r", Backspace "\x7f", Tab "\t" only in a multi-line field, as the OSK); characters without a US key as code 0 + UTF-8 (as the OSK's euro / pound / yen keys) | one path for both |
| D10 | `vkb=zone` exactly while the launcher is in front; any fullscreen app in front (all run turned in S3) publishes the usual rule; `APPLAUNCH_OSK=force` does not show the keyboard over the zone | the brief's input rule |
| D11 | The zone is switched on by `APPLAUNCH_PDA=1` (the board profile); a turned app inherits it but gets a landscape canvas, where `cp0_zone_layout()` refuses (portrait only), so a future zone-aware backend in it draws nothing | 038 section 4's flag; no per-launch override needed |
| D12 | `glyphs-extra.txt` (accents, rarer characters) is loaded by default (`APPLAUNCH_STROKES_EXTRA=0` leaves it out) | the brief lists dead-key accents among what the zone types |
| D13 | Time base: the frames' CLOCK_MONOTONIC event times and `cp0_touch_now_us()`; the LVGL tick when the device refuses `EVIOCSCLOCKID` (the harness's pipe: deterministic) | one clock for the heal window, taps and polling |
| D14 | Home: the switcher's own Home when it is up, else a page closes as with Esc held (`go_back_home`), else the home grid. Apps: opens the switcher as G1 does, closes it when up, nothing (logged) when multitasking is off. Menu: Ctrl+O press and release | 038's soft-button mapping |
| D15 | The screensaver (clock) draws the band black; its touches are swallowed by the router | the overlay covers only the app area |

## 10. Risks and open points

1. **Real stylus accuracy** is unknown (S1's thresholds come from synthetic strokes); the strokes log (D17) is the way
   to measure it.
2. **Repaint speed** on the Pi 3A+ (the CPU turns every changed area): the ink redraws a small area per frame; the strip
   toggling redraws the band and a strip of the app area.
3. **The 5 mm soft buttons and keys** (D1) are under the 7 mm TARGET rule: fine with a stylus, small for a finger.
4. **The home grid** draws a 1-2 px sliver of its next row just above the band (its grid places rows by a remainder
   rule that predates S3; with pda.5 at 480x800 too, the 480x482 area leaves 1 px). Not changed here.
5. **The Square class of the app area** makes page headers 64 px high on the PDA at 480x800 (56 in S2); at 480x640 the
   app area was Compact in pda.5 and is Square again in pda.6 (item 10).
6. **Screens that do not follow the keyboard inset** (the home grid, compat pages with their toolbar) are partly
   covered while the strip is open.
7. **Menu (Ctrl+O)** acts on the launcher's own pages only today (no page uses Ctrl+O); it is meant for portrait apps
   (S4). A turned app has no band, so no Menu.
8. **The lock screen of the stock build** (`ui_screensaver.cpp`, not the Pi's native screensaver) does not blank the band.
9. **A future zone-aware app process** inherits `APPLAUNCH_PDA=1`; S4 must coordinate who draws the band (D11 only covers
   turned apps).
10. **480x640 with the zone a third of the panel** (pda.5): the app area 480x374 is under the 400 px of the Square class,
    so it is Compact: 40 px header, page titles at 24 px, and "On-screen keyboard" is cut ("On-screen keyb..."). The
    reference panel (480x800) is not affected. Fixed in pda.6 by the cap of section 12.3 (the app area 480x400, the
    writing zone 187 px there).
11. **d under the harsh generator** loses about 6 points (75.0 to 69.0 %, mostly rejected) to the new b variants, which
    share its stem and retrace (section 12.1).

### 12.1 b and V: root cause and fix

Natural shapes, 300 samples each (default generator; harsh in brackets where it matters), correct / wrong %:

| Shape | pda.4 | pda.5 |
|---|---|---|
| V without the flick (plain) | 2 / 69 (read as u; the rest refused, u the best candidate) | 98.7 / 0.7 |

| the chart's V (the shipped template) | 99.7 (harsh 78.7) | 99.7 (harsh 87.3) |
| lower-case b (stem down, back up half way, a bowl to the right) | 0 / 13 (refused, d the best candidate; or d) | 99.7 / 0 |
| lower-case b with a wide bowl; b with its bowl drawn on from the foot of the stem | 0 / 7 (d); 0 / 0 (refused, p) | 98.7; 99.7 |

| the chart's B (the shipped template) | 98.7 (harsh 60.7) | 99.3 (harsh 63.0) |
| u: the chart's, a wide one (in no table), a narrow one | 100, 100, 74 / 0 | 100, 100, 96.3 / 0.7 |

- **V.** The table had one v: the chart's, whose long flick to the right at the top (about 40 % of the glyph's width,
  a sixth of its path) is what the chart uses to keep V apart from U. The matcher compares the 32-point shapes (cosine,
  points in order) and had no feature for a sharp point, so a V written without the flick is closer to the round u than
  to the flicked v: typed u (69 %) or refused. The capital itself was never the problem: the shift line is recognised
  (99.5 %), the v after it was not.
- **b.** The table had one b: the chart's B (stem down, back up the stem, two bumps). Its stem and retrace, almost half
  the path, are d's too, so every natural b (the lower-case b, a bigger lower bump, the B written up from the bottom,
  the b drawn on from the foot of the stem) lands nearer d or p than the one template: refused (d best) or typed d.
  The chart's B copied closely works (98.7 %) but was the weakest letter under the harsh generator (66.5 %). The pair
  b / 6 never meets: 6 is a digits-box glyph, the letters box does not consider it; k was not involved (99.5 % before,
  100 % after).
- **Not the box.** The recogniser scales each stroke uniformly to its own bounding square; the box only says letters or
  digits. The same shapes give the same results 8-16 mm and 15-28 mm high (the taller zone's sizes; tests below).
- **Fix (Strokes 0.2.1).** A stroke feature, the sharpest turn (the largest turning over three consecutive of its 32
  points: a V's point 130-180 degrees, a u's round bottom 50-110), and a gate on it, `sharp=MIN` or `sharp=MIN-MAX`
  (`strokes_geom.c`, `strokes_match.c`, `strokes_table.c`; the README's gate table; an older library refuses a table
  that uses it). The shipped table keeps each glyph's chart stroke first and adds natural variants: b 6 (the lower-case
  b, with a wide bowl, drawn on from the foot of the stem; the chart's B with a full stem, with a bigger lower bump,
  written up the stem from the bottom), v 3 (a plain V `sharp=130`, a short flick `sharp=120`, a wide V `sharp=110`),
  u 1 (a narrow u `start=tl sharp=0-120`, so a narrow u is not taken for the new V and an o open at the top stays an
  o). 93 glyphs, 103 templates; the optional section unchanged.

Accuracy, the gate of `make eval` (default generator, 200 per glyph, seed 1; the samples come from the table, so the
new variants are written too), correct / rejected / wrong %:

| | pda.4 | pda.5 |
